# Session 5 — daily beta(t) restored

## What changed and why

You reinstated the 365-point curve as the primary estimand. The reason is decisive:
the project has to tell an irrigator *which week* to release water, and a
four-window model can only say "summer". The seasonal model stays, with a defined
job — the powered confirmatory test of whether flow matters at all — while beta(t)
says when.

## The thing I got wrong, and the thing it led to

Working through what a manager actually does with beta(t), it is not the curve they
need. beta(t) is the effect of a one-SD standardized log anomaly; they release cfs.
The conversion is

    benefit per cfs  =  beta(t) / (sigma * Qbar(t))

and because baseline discharge at Norris spans more than an order of magnitude
across the year, dividing by Qbar(t) **changes the shape of the curve**. The same 10
cfs is a ~6% proportional increase at September base flow and ~0.5% at the June
snowmelt peak — thirteen times the effect from identical water and identical beta.

So there are two curves, and both go in the paper:

- **beta(t)** — the ecological curve. RQ2.
- **s(t) = beta(t)/(sigma·Qbar(t))** — the management curve. RQ1.

Figure 6a plots them together, scaled, so the difference is visible. I think this is
a genuine contribution rather than a technicality — the functional flow-ecology
literature reports beta(t) and stops.

## How precisely you may speak

Not by finding the peak of the fitted curve. Script 06 simulates 4,000 whole curves
from the full uncertainty and records which day wins each time. The 80% interval of
that vote is the resolution, and its width is set by your record, not by a modelling
choice. `output/tables/best_day_intervals.csv` has a `resolvable` column; where it
is FALSE, no date goes anywhere in the manuscript.

## What was built

Nine scripts (was six), plus `R/fn_fit_beta.R` holding the only two custom fitting
functions. New: beta_S(t) with its own lag sweep, production sensitivity via a
73-block paired simulation sweep with the pathway interaction made explicit, and BoR
scenarios decomposed **by day** so you can say *why* a scenario hurts.

Website: `manuscript/` renders three pages to `_site/`, published to `gh-pages` by
`publish_site.sh`. Rendering is local by necessity — a CI runner has no data.

## One thing you must edit before Figure 9a means anything

`LIFE` in `R/09_figures.R` holds placeholder life-stage windows (spawning,
incubation, emergence, first summer, first winter). Figure 9a is the RQ2 result and
it is only as good as that timeline. Replace with MFWP redd-survey timing and
